PRIVACY POLICY

THE BEAUTIFUL UNITY GYMNASIUM · EFFECTIVE 08.31.2026

This policy states what the apparatus collects, why it collects it, and what it does with it afterward. It is written to be read rather than to be scrolled past.

01THE ENTITY

Samuel George Klickner, trading as The Beautiful Unity Gymnasium, Portland, Oregon. Correspondence to b.u.gymnasium@gmail.com.

The entity is the data controller for everything described below.

02WHAT IS COLLECTED

Only what is submitted deliberately. There is no account system, no profile, and nothing is gathered in the background beyond ordinary server logs.

  • Email address — if submitted to the subscription field.
  • Mobile number — if submitted to the subscription field for text dispatches.
  • Message content — whatever is written in correspondence sent to the address above.

Nothing else is requested. The site sells nothing directly and therefore takes no payment information; purchases are handled by third parties on their own platforms under their own policies.

03WHAT IS DONE WITH IT

  • Sending release bulletins, program notices and institutional dispatches.
  • Answering correspondence.
  • Keeping the subscription list accurate.

Dispatches are scarce by design. There is no arrangement under which contact information is sold, rented, traded, or handed to anyone for their own marketing.

04TEXT MESSAGES

Text dispatches are opt-in. A number is added only when submitted to the subscription field on this site, and can be removed at any time.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service, is permitted. All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Message frequency varies and is not expected to exceed a small number of messages per release cycle. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel or HELP for assistance. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

05WHO ELSE SEES IT

The apparatus is small and runs on other people's infrastructure. Contact information is held by the services that make delivery possible:

  • Mailchimp (Intuit) — email and text dispatch, and the subscription list itself.
  • Vercel — hosting; ordinary server logs including IP address and request time.

These are processors acting on instruction, not recipients of a transfer. Information may also be disclosed where compelled by law, and only to the extent compelled.

06HOW LONG IT IS KEPT

Contact information is retained until removal is requested, and is deleted on request without condition. Unsubscribing removes an address from dispatch; asking for deletion removes it entirely. Correspondence is kept only as long as it is useful to answer.

07WITHDRAWAL

Every dispatch carries an unsubscribe link. Text dispatches stop on STOP. Either may also be ended by writing to b.u.gymnasium@gmail.com, which is read by a person.

Withdrawal is a transfer, not a vacancy: the record leaves the register and nothing is retained in its place.

08RIGHTS

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see what is held about you, to correct it, to have it deleted, to object to its use, to receive a copy in a portable format, or to withdraw consent already given. All of these are exercised the same way: by writing to the address above. Requests are answered; they are not charged for.

Residents of the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom may also complain to their national supervisory authority.

09LEGAL BASIS

Where a legal basis is required, it is consent — given by submitting an address or number — and, for answering correspondence and keeping records accurate, legitimate interest.

10PLACE

The entity operates in the United States and its processors store data there. Information submitted from elsewhere is transferred to and held in the United States.

11CHILDREN

The Services are not directed at anyone under 13, and information is not knowingly collected from them. Anything collected in error is deleted on notice.

12AMENDMENT

This policy may be revised. The effective date at the head of the document is the operative one. Material changes will be announced through the same dispatch apparatus this policy governs.
